## Limits and unselected scope

Navigation coverage is sampled. Sitemap/robots discovery was not performed in this execution. Repeated catalog pages and all linked content were not crawled. An unvisited link does not establish a complete feature. Login completion and external mutations were excluded.

Video, message entry, save/favorite functions and many footer tools were discovered through labels/links but not exercised. Map tile rendering, completed comparison, filter combinations, AI search behavior and authenticated flows remain unverified. Exact legal, marketing and seller content truth was outside scope.
